# Website outcomes

- [x] **Bilingual responsive temple website** — English is the initial/default view. A visible `English | தமிழ்` switch appears on desktop and mobile; it changes visitor-facing content without navigation and remembers the preference in browser storage when available. Navigation, page titles, welcome copy, section copy, history notes, gallery filters/captions, directions, footer and accessibility labels have English and Tamil versions. Tamil phrasing is suggested and should be reviewed by the temple.
- [x] **Temple home and deity information** — The site presents `Arulmigu Sri Kamatchi Amman Temple` and `அருள்மிகு ஸ்ரீ காமாட்சி அம்மன் திருக்கோயில்`, a welcome message, supplied deity photo and links to History, Gallery and Visit. Sri Kamatchi Amman / Sanctum and Other Deities sections use supplied corresponding photos. It does not invent worship details, timings, festivals, dates, consecration details or unsupported claims.
- [x] **History and heritage** — The three enhanced history photographs supplied in the follow-up replace the earlier collage, inscription and framed-portrait images in the History section and gallery. The requester confirmed the inscription is verified; no confirmation warning is displayed. No additional dates, events, biography or current-status claims have been invented.
- [x] **Gallery** — Supplied photos are grouped into Temple Exterior, Main Deity & Sanctum, Other Deities, and History & Heritage, with English/Tamil category labels and captions, mobile-friendly display, accessible image alt text, category filters, and an enlarged-image lightbox. Only supplied temple photos are used; originals were copied unchanged.
- [x] **Visit information** — The directions actions use the exact URL `https://maps.app.goo.gl/Qc5W1fos4k9ztnnu7?g_st=iw`, labeled `Get directions` in English and `வழிகாட்டியைப் பெறுங்கள்` in Tamil. No address, opening hours or email have been invented.
- [x] **Special events and contact** — A bilingual `Special events` section states `No special events at this time` in English and `தற்போது சிறப்பு நிகழ்வுகள் எதுவும் இல்லை` in Tamil; it says dates and timings will be added when available and invents no schedule. A bilingual contact section lists `Sivakumar — +91 6382 182 841` and `Athith — +91 7358218611` as tap-to-call links.
- [x] **Visual quality, accessibility, and deployment readiness** — The site uses the supplied South Indian heritage direction and palette (`#702522`, `#A67C38`, `#F7F0E4`, `#30201D`, `#E5D6BB`), Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and Noto Sans Tamil, a responsive layout, semantic sections, keyboard focus styles, reduced-motion support, keyboard-operable navigation/gallery/lightbox and descriptive image alt text. It is a self-contained React/TypeScript/Vite project configured for later GitHub/Vercel use, with no publishing, external service connection, API keys, or custom payment backend. Supplied handoff images were retained unedited as local website copies.
- [x] **Footer** — The footer includes the temple name, navigation to the events and contact sections, language switch and directions.
